THE RULES OF THIS LITTLE WORLD

Three paths to the same arc.

Each launcher turns your inputs into an initial velocity. After release, the same gravity acts on every ball. Nothing fires again during flight.

01 — Slingshot

An ideal linear spring is drawn a fixed 0.50 m. You set the force at full draw and the launch angle. The band’s force decreases to zero as it relaxes, so its average force is half the peak force.

E = ½Fpeakd   →   v = √(Fpeakd / m)

All stored elastic energy becomes the ball’s kinetic energy. The model neglects the band’s mass and losses.

02 — Jetpack ball

Two ideal instantaneous impulses act together at launch: Jₓ and Jᵧ, measured in newton-seconds (N·s). Positive X points right; positive Y points up. Negative inputs point the opposite way.

vₓ = Jₓ / m   ·   vᵧ = Jᵧ / m

The ball starts at rest. The jetpack is treated as massless; fuel loss and burn duration are ignored. An impulse is a change in momentum, not a force.

03 — Falling weight

A counterweight of mass M falls through height h. An ideal mechanism transfers all the lost gravitational potential energy to the ball. A lossless guide sets the ball’s exit direction.

Mgh = ½mv²   →   v = √(2Mgh / m)

This is an ideal energy-transfer model, not a simulated hammer collision: the weight ends at rest, moving parts are massless, and there is no friction. Drop height refers to the weight’s travel, not the ball’s launch height.

The shared court

The ball’s mass m = 0.60 kg and radius is 0.12 m. It launches from (0, 1.20) m relative to the marked origin. The hoop is 10.00 m away, 3.05 m above the floor, with a 0.45 m opening.

x = vₓt   ·   y = 1.20 + vᵧt − ½gt²

Gravity is 9.81 m/s²; there is no air resistance, spin lift, or wind. Angles are counterclockwise from +X: 0° is right, 90° is up. A basket counts only when the entire ball fits through the hoop while moving downward.

Collisions use approximate restitution coefficients: floor 0.72, rim 0.65, and backboard 0.78. A floor bounce retains 82% of horizontal speed. The net is visual only. Floor and bank shots count. Attempts end after the ball settles, leaves the horizontal play area, or reaches 12 simulated seconds.
